# Public Content and Interaction Contract

## Public Surfaces

| Surface | Required message | Preserved interface |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | All three intended benefits and development status | Brand, navigation, CTA destinations |
| `/technology/` | Observations/records, AI review, human decisions and intended follow-up | Three-stage conceptual walkthrough |
| `/about/` | Practical purpose, responsible development, no outcome promises | Existing page structure and imagery |
| `/contact/` | Relevant inquiry context and development status | Existing inquiry controls and email destination |
| Shared FAQ/CTA/footer | No conflicting readiness or purpose claims | Existing controls, navigation and disclosure semantics |
| Metadata/search previews | Purpose qualified as in development | Existing canonical addresses and brand title |

## Wording Boundaries

- Documentation: intended reduction of repeated entry and support for useful records;
  no claim of measured savings or currently working report generation.
- AI: suggestions for qualified human review; may be incomplete or incorrect;
  no guaranteed hazard detection, work authorization, or site-safety certification.
- Follow-up: intended support for organizing responsibilities and reviewing progress;
  no claim that notifications, assignments, approvals, or verified closure are implemented.
- Exclude internal requirement IDs, acceptance thresholds, pilot parties/arrangements, dates,
  budgets, commercial terms, internal decisions and source documents from public output.
- General collaboration invitations remain permissible without agreed-pilot implications.

## Interaction Invariants

- Preserve menu operation, Escape dismissal, navigation, FAQ expansion and reduced motion.
- Preserve workflow tab roles, labels, IDs, panel associations, focus and keyboard behavior.
- Preserve inquiry validation, preview, recipient, subject/body format, encoded mailto,
  copy/download options, stale-draft clearing and honest status announcements.
- Do not add automatic sending, collection, persistence, telemetry, or external resources.
- Keep all existing routes, imagery disclosures, logos and palette; small copy-wrapping fixes
  must retain layout and accessibility at 320 px width and desktop.

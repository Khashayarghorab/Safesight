SafeSight — Brand Pack

PRIMARY STORY
SafeSight helps workers see risk before it becomes an accident.

LOGO
The approved mark is the geometric S symbol supplied in this pack.
Use the primary black version on light backgrounds and the white version on dark backgrounds.
Keep the symbol proportions unchanged. Do not stretch, rotate, outline, or add effects.

PRIMARY COLORS
Onyx Black
HEX: #000000
RGB: 0, 0, 0

Pure White
HEX: #FFFFFF
RGB: 255, 255, 255

Safety Yellow — accent only
HEX: #FFC107
RGB: 255, 193, 7

COLOR USE
Black and white are the core identity.
Use Safety Yellow sparingly for calls-to-action, highlights, hazard/safety signals, and key UI states.

WORDMARK
The supplied SafeSight wordmark artwork is the approved wordmark.
For consistency, use the SVG/PNG wordmark assets instead of retyping the logo text.

CLEAR SPACE
Keep clear space around the mark equal to approximately 20% of the icon width whenever possible.

MINIMUM DIGITAL SIZE
Primary icon: 16 px minimum.
Horizontal logo: 120 px wide minimum where readability matters.

FILES
SVG = preferred for websites, print, presentations, and scalable use.
PNG = transparent-background raster exports for apps, slides, documents, and social media.

All PNG assets in this pack have transparent backgrounds unless a mockup/reference sheet is explicitly included.
